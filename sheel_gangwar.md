Big Data refers to extremely large, complex and continuously growing datasets that are difficult to store, process, and analyze using traditinal data processing methods .It is generated from many different sources such as social media, websites, mobile devices, etc.
The 5 characteristics of big data are:                                                                                                   Volume: The enormous amount of data generated and collected.                                                                                             Velocity: The high speed at which data is generated,stored and processed.                                                                                             Vareity: The different types of data such as text, images, video, and numerical data.                                                                                                  Veracity: The accuracy ,reliability, and quality of data.                                                                                                  Value: The useful information and insights that can be obtained from the data.
Since traditional systems may not be sufficient to handle such large and complex datasets, technologies such as Hadoop and Apache Spark are used to store and process Big Data efficiently.
in simple terms, BIG DATA is about handling massive amounts of diverse and rapidly generated data and turning it into useful information and insights.
