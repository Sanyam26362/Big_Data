Big Data refers to extremely large, fast-growing, and complex sets of data that traditional data-processing tools struggle to handle efficiently. It isn't just about having "a lot of data" — it's about the unique challenges and opportunities that come from working with information at massive scale, arriving continuously from many different sources and in many different formats.

5 V's of Big Data
Volume — Bahut zyada amount of data, jo TB, PB ya EB mein ho sakta hai.

Velocity — Data kitni fast generate aur process hota hai, jaise online transactions.

Variety — Data alag-alag forms mein hota hai, jaise text, images, videos, JSON, etc.

Veracity — Data kitna accurate aur reliable hai. Raw data mein errors ya missing information ho sakti hai.

Value — Data se useful information aur insights nikalna, jisse better decisions liye ja sakein.
