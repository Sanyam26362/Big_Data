# What is Big Data?

**Big Data** refers to extremely large, complex, and fast-growing datasets that traditional data processing systems cannot efficiently store, manage, or analyze.

### The 5 Vs of Big Data
* **Volume:** Massive amounts of data generated every second (terabytes to petabytes).
* **Velocity:** The speed at which new data is generated, collected, and processed in real time.
* **Variety:** Diverse data formats, including structured (SQL tables), semi-structured (JSON, XML), and unstructured (text, video, audio).
* **Veracity:** The accuracy, trustworthiness, and quality of the collected data.
* **Value:** The actionable insights and business outcomes extracted from the data.

### Common Applications
* Predictive analytics and machine learning model training.
* Real-time recommendation engines (e.g., Netflix, Spotify).
* Financial fraud detection and risk assessment.
