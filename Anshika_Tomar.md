What is Big Data?
<br>
Big Data refers to extremely large and complex datasets that cannot be efficiently processed using traditional data-processing methods.
<br>
Characteristics of Big Data:
<br>
The major characteristics of Big Data include:
<br>
-Volume
<br>
-Velocity
<br>
Variety
<br>
Veracity
<br>
Value
<br>
Examples:
<br>
Examples of Big Data include social media data, online transactions, sensor data, healthcare records, and website activity.
<br>
Applications:
<br>
Big Data is used in healthcare, banking, e-commerce, weather forecasting, transportation and many other fields.
<br>
Conclusion:
<br>
Big Data helps organizations analyze large amounts of information and make better decisions.
