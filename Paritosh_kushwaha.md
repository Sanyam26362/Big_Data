# Big Data - 

**Big Data** refers to extremely large, complex, and rapidly generated amounts of data that cannot be efficiently stored, processed, or analyzed using traditional data-processing methods. It is generated from various sources such as social media, online transactions, smartphones, websites, sensors, GPS devices, healthcare systems, and financial transactions.
