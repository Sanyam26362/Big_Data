#What is Big Data?
---
Big data refers to extremely large, complex data sets that grow continuously and cannot be handled by traditional data processing tools. The raw data from many apps, Iot centers, Social media and transaction logs is stored in scalable systems such as Cloud Data Lakes.

## Characteristics of Big Data
---
* Generation of large sums of data every second.
* The data is processed at a very high speed

##Applications
---
*Retail services
*Finances
*Goverment
