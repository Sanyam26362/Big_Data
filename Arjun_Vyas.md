--> BIG DATA

Big Data refers to extremely large and complex collections of data that cannot be efficiently processed using traditional data processing tools. This data can come from various sources such as social media, online transactions, sensors, websites, mobile applications, and business systems.

BIG DATA MEANS -->
A huge amount of data is generated every day.
Data is generated and processed at a very high speed.
Data exists in different forms such as text, images, videos, audio, and structured or unstructured data.
Technologies such as Hadoop, Apache Spark, and distributed databases help organizations store, process, and analyze Big Data. Big Data is useful in areas such as healthcare, business, finance, social media, artificial intelligence, and scientific research.
In simple words, Big Data helps organizations analyze massive amounts of information and use it to make better decisions.
