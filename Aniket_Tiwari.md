\# What is Big Data?



Big Data refers to extremely large and complex amounts of data that are

generated from sources such as social media, websites, sensors, mobile

applications, and online transactions.



Traditional data processing tools may struggle to store, process, and

analyze such huge datasets. Big Data technologies help organizations

collect, store, process, and analyze this information efficiently.



Big Data is commonly described using the 5 Vs:



\- Volume – the huge amount of data generated.

\- Velocity – the speed at which data is generated and processed.

\- Variety – different types and formats of data.

\- Veracity – the accuracy and reliability of data.

\- Value – the useful information that can be obtained from the data.



Big Data is used in areas such as healthcare, banking, e-commerce,

social media, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence.
