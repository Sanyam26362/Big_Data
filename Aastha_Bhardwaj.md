#What is Big Data?
Big Data is the large amount of data and databases which cannot be handled easily.
It is defined by 5V's
Volume -- Large amount of data that is generated.
Velocity -- The rate of speed at which data is generated.
Variety -- Different forms of data such as images,videos and texts.
Veracity -- The accuracy and reliability of data.
Value -- Information obtained from data generated.

It gathers information from apps and sensors and stores massive files in large database systems.
